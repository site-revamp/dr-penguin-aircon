# Dr Penguin Aircon Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Dr Penguin Aircon provides servicing, maintenance, repairs, and installation for homes and businesses in Singapore. The homepage draft helps visitors compare cleaning options, check service conditions, and request a quote.

## Main Improvements

1. **Make prices and surcharges easier to find.** The draft groups the dedicated service page’s standard cleaning rates by unit count, lists optional add-ons separately, and shows evening, Sunday, and public-holiday surcharges. The advertised S$40 starting price and competing promotional rates still need reconciliation before publication.
2. **Give experience, reviews, and warranty claims context.** The draft attributes the experience claim, displayed Google rating, and advertised 90-day workmanship warranty to the company and prompts customers to confirm warranty coverage. The rating still needs a direct review-profile link, and the experience claim, project imagery, and seven-day follow-up terms need verification.
3. **Help visitors choose a service and request a quote.** Service cards distinguish routine servicing, deeper cleaning, repairs, parts replacement, and installation. Pricing notes and FAQs clarify standard-cleaning eligibility and location exclusions. The enquiry form captures service, unit count, and property type, with preferred timing entered in the message field. It opens a prefilled WhatsApp message, and a fixed mobile bar keeps Call and WhatsApp within reach.

## Design Improvements

Consistent service cards, a separate add-on list, and a four-step enquiry-to-follow-up section make it easier to scan options and understand what happens after making contact.
