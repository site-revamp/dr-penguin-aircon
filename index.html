Helpful information

Frequently asked questions

Which aircon brands do you support?

The company website mentions support for brands including Panasonic, Daikin and Mitsubishi. Contact the team to confirm support for your model.

Is the standard cleaning package for all property types?

The dedicated package page states that the offer is for HDB and condominium residential units, and wall-mounted units only. Other requirements should be quoted separately.

Are there additional charges for evening appointments?

The service page states that a S$30 per-trip surcharge may apply after 6 PM from Monday to Saturday, and S$50 per trip on Sundays or public holidays.

What if my aircon is leaking or not cooling?

Contact the team with the symptoms and unit details. The appropriate inspection or repair option can be discussed before work proceeds.

Do you provide a workmanship warranty?

The website states a free 90-day workmanship warranty. Ask the team to confirm the scope, exclusions and claim process for your specific job.

Do you service every area in Singapore?

The standard servicing page lists Sentosa and Tuas as excluded areas. Confirm availability for your address before making an appointment.
